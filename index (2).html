How it's modeled: channel commission and the TRACK Distribution fee are both taken as a percentage of the amount actually collected — that is, the rate after the discount — and are additive, not compounded. The distribution fee applies on every channel regardless of who collects the payment. Taxes and cleaning are treated as pass-through and excluded.

Markup is applied to a $1,000 base rate. The result scales linearly, so the same percentage holds at any reservation value.
